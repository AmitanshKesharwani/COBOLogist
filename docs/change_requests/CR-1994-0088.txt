Date:       1994-03-11
Submitted:  R. Haskell, Policy Administration
Approved:   D. Morrow, VP Operations  (signed 1994-03-14)

SUBJECT: Grace period extension for long-tenured policyholders upon lapse

BACKGROUND:
Claims dept has flagged repeat complaints from policyholders with 5+ years
continuous coverage who miss renewal due to address change or banking error
and are denied on a lapse technicality. Attrition data shows these are
low-fraud, low-risk accounts.

CHANGE:
If policy lapsed within 30 days of claim date AND policyholder held
continuous coverage > 5 years prior to lapse, treat claim as payable.
No change to fraud review procedure. 30-day window non-negotiable per
actuarial sign-off (E. Briggs, 1994-03-10).

IMPLEMENTATION:
Add paragraph to CLAIMS-PROC module. Effective for claims received
on or after 1994-04-01.
