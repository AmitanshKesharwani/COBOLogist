INTERNAL COMPLIANCE MEMO
Date:    2003-09-22
From:    Legal / Regulatory Affairs
To:      Claims Systems Development
Re:      NY State Consent Decree -- Implementation Requirement

Per settlement agreement with the New York State Insurance Department
(index no. 03-INS-0471, finalized 2003-09-08), the company is required
to process claims from New York-domiciled policyholders as payable even
when the incident description field is not completed, provided all other
material claim fields are present.

Requirement is effective immediately for all NY-state claims.
Implement as a pre-eligibility override in the claims adjudication
program. Do not apply to any other state code.
